Privacy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

Namey is a baby-naming app. This page explains what we collect and what we don’t.

Your shortlist stays on your phone

Names you save (favourites) are stored on this device. We don’t create an account for you to use Namey, and we don’t sync your shortlist to our servers.

Optional Pro waitlist email

If you join the Pro waitlist, we keep your email in a private Google Sheet and only use it to tell you when Pro opens. You can ignore the waitlist and keep using Namey without giving an email.

No sale of data

We don’t sell your personal data. We don’t use your shortlist for advertising profiles.

Tips and payments

Tips may come later. This version does not take payments. If that changes, we’ll update this page before any payment is offered.

What we don’t do

  • We don’t require an account to browse or save names
  • We don’t track you around other websites for ads
  • We don’t mix Namey data with other products

Meanings and origins

Meanings and origins are given as-is for inspiration. They are not certified history or a guarantee of accuracy.

Contact

Questions, or want your waitlist email deleted? Email edwardflavel@gmail.com and we'll remove it.

Changes

If we change how Namey handles data, we’ll update this page and the date above.
