## Full description

```
Find the name that feels like them.

Namey helps expectant parents shortlist baby names with meanings you’ll actually remember — without another endless list, and without making an account.

How it works
• Choose boy or girl
• Add any cultures or themes you like
• Answer a short quiz (skip any you’re not sure about)
• Browse names that fit — tap for origin, meaning, and a deeper hidden meaning
• Save favourites on this phone and share a name with family or on TikTok

Built for tired parents
• Clear, plain English
• Shortlist stays on your phone — no account required
• Soft “Add to Home Screen” so Namey is one tap away
• Share a name and its meaning in one go

Namey Pro
Pro is on the way. You can join the waitlist in the app. There is no payment in this version.

Privacy in one line
Your favourites stay on this device. We don’t sell your data.

Start naming — and keep the ones that feel right.
```

## Privacy policy (public page body)

Title suggestion for the page: **Privacy** · Last updated: 11 September 2026

```
Privacy

Last updated: 11 September 2026

Namey is a baby-naming app. This page explains what we collect and what we don’t.

Your shortlist stays on your phone
Names you save (favourites) are stored on this device. We don’t create an account for you to use Namey, and we don’t sync your shortlist to our servers.

Optional Pro waitlist email
If you join the Namey Pro waitlist, you can leave an email address so we can tell you when Pro opens. We use that email only for waitlist messages about Namey Pro. You can ignore the waitlist and keep using Namey without giving an email.

No sale of data
We don’t sell your personal data. We don’t use your shortlist for advertising profiles.

Tips and payments
Tips may come later. This version does not take payments. If that changes, we’ll update this page before any payment is offered.

Name meanings and origins
Meanings and origins in Namey are given as-is for inspiration. They are not certified history or a guarantee of accuracy.

What we don’t do
• We don’t require an account to browse or save names
• We don’t track you around other websites for ads
• We don’t mix Namey data with other products

Contact
Questions about privacy: use the contact details on the Play listing or the Namey site (https://eflav.github.io/namey/).

Changes
If we change how Namey handles data, we’ll update this page and the date above.
```
